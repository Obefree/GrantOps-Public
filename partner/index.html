Partner requirements

A practical checklist for a company joining a grant application as a consortium partner, beneficiary or technical work-package participant.

Basic company requirements

For the first eligibility check, we normally need:

Legal entity
Registered company in an eligible country.
Legal details
Official name, registered address, company and VAT/tax number.
PIC
Funding & Tenders Portal Participant Identification Code where EU central programmes require it.
SME status
Where the call has SME-specific eligibility or advantages.
Financials
Recent financial statements and ability to pass call-specific financial-capacity checks.
Compliance
No exclusion, bankruptcy or call-blocking tax/social-security issues.
Team & capability
People and technical capacity relevant to the proposed work package.
Project budget
Ability to define tasks, person-months, costs, milestones and deliverables.
Co-financing
Ability to cover the non-funded share where the grant is below 100%.
Past projects
EU/national grant experience is useful, but not always mandatory.

Minimum information to send us

  • Legal company name
  • Country of registration
  • Company / VAT number
  • SME status
  • PIC, if already available
  • Latest financial statements
  • Short description of team and technical capabilities
  • Previous EU-funded projects, if any

This is enough for an initial screening. Exact documentation depends on the specific programme and call.

Main grant routes involving partners

Quick orientation before looking at individual calls.

Horizon EuropeRIA / IA collaborative calls
Partners required
Usually a multi-country consortium. A technology company can participate as beneficiary, technical WP lead or implementation partner.
Digital Europeincluding EdTech and information-integrity routes
Partners required
Typically international deployment consortia. Strong route for product, infrastructure, AI/data and technical delivery roles.
Eurostarsinternational R&D
Partners required
International R&D consortium, generally SME-led under programme rules.
Creative EuropeMedia Literacy / Journalism Partnerships
Partners required
Cross-border consortium, normally with media, NGO, education or cultural organisations; company can be technical partner.
CERVselected calls
Call-specific
Some routes require cross-country partners or a nonprofit lead; company participation depends on the exact topic.
Portugal 2030 / COMPETEBusiness R&D / demonstrators
Individual or co-promotion
A Portuguese company may apply alone in eligible individual routes or participate with companies/research organisations in co-promotion.
EIC Accelerator
Single applicant
Primarily a direct startup/SME route rather than a consortium route.
