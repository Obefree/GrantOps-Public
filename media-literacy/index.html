Why Faking News?

Faking News is a gamified critical-thinking and media-literacy learning concept for children and teenagers; CCR (Contextual Critical Reading) is a related explainable critical-reading methodology, technology, and research platform. We are exploring educational investigations based on claims, sources, evidence, context, and uncertainty — not only binary “fake/real” quizzes.

For schools & youth programmes

Faking News Youth

Interactive news investigations, branching feedback, progressive practice and teacher-friendly assessments. Proposed primary pilot audience: ages 12–16.

Proposed product direction; not a claim of independent clinical or educational validation.
For community & research

CCR / Community learning

Contextual explanations, real-world reading tasks, multilingual experiments and evaluation. Potentially suitable for adult learning, public libraries and media-literacy organisations.

Target audience and work packages depend on the specific funded programme.

What does research show?

Results depend on the game, study design, measures and time elapsed since playing. Short-term improvement does not automatically mean durable real-world critical-thinking gains.

Work with us — paid delivery and research collaboration

We are interested in defined, paid implementation tasks inside existing programmes, as well as clearly scoped pilot partnerships and new research consortia. Funding or available budgets have not been confirmed with the organisations above.

Interactive learning prototypes

Branching learning scenarios, serious-game mechanics, decision feedback, learner progress, and browser/mobile prototypes.

Research & evaluation tooling

Pre/post tests, control-group flows, anonymised learning-event collection, repeat-practice experiments and exportable metrics.

Content and language adaptation

Interactive media-literacy exercises, annotated examples, localization support and editorially reviewed datasets.

Training and workshops

Facilitated sessions, game-based critical-thinking workshops, teacher-training support and pilot implementation.

How to enter a funded projectAsk its coordinator whether the approved work plan permits a procurement or subcontract for a concrete deliverable. Adding a new beneficiary is different and may require a grant amendment. Do not assume unspent grant funds are freely distributable.
