SPS V9 prototyping voucher is now open

€500–5,000 · 60% · digital prototypes are eligible.

Important gate: the applicant must be a Slovenian SME entered in the Register of Innovative Startup Companies, have at least one employee, and complete the mandatory National Startup Consortium diagnostic interview before applying. The prototype must not already be completed before submission.

First action: verify this eligibility route locally before spending money or preparing the prototype application.
